ABOUT GSC

GSC Garments is an independent streetwear brand built around a simple idea: simple clothes, big dreams.

We make clean, oversized essentials with a darker edge. Every piece carries the GSC mark and a star as a signature, and every design is made to be worn every day without making noise.

Drop 01 starts with the T-Shirt Core: a black oversized tee with a small chest logo on the front and a full back design built around a mountain and a set of coordinates. It is where the brand starts, and there is more to come.

Based in Valencia, Spain.
